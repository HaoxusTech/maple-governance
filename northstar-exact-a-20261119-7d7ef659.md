# Proposed Northstar demo release-readiness requirement

This proposed requirement applies only to creating or updating release-readiness issues for the fictional Northstar demo `exact-a-20261119-7d7ef659` in `HaoxusTech/maple-workspace`. Other demos, services and issue operations remain outside its scope.

The resulting issue body must include both:

- An explicit named synthetic owner, such as a person, team or role; an unspecified or pending owner is insufficient.
- A link to the corresponding synthetic release brief.

For this demo, an example owner is `Northstar Release Team` and the brief is https://github.com/HaoxusTech/maple-workspace/blob/main/docs/northstar/exact-a-20261119-7d7ef659.md.

For requests in scope, reject with a clear explanation if either field is missing or the resulting issue body cannot be inspected. When approved and selected for enforcement, this scoped requirement takes precedence over the general development allow-all policy in rules.md; that policy remains unchanged outside this scope.

These fields establish review readiness only, not successful validation, release approval or production readiness. This proposal does not modify any existing issue or retroactively assess its creation.

Independent human review and merge are required before this proposal is eligible for selection as part of an immutable approved requirements revision. Publication, local edits and development allow-all decisions do not activate this requirement or constitute that review.
